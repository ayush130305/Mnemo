Each piece is one 1 KB page after compression; each square is a 128-byte chunk. The chip's allocator can place chunks anywhere, so the shapes are only a picture of the packing problem. A page is compressed only if it saves at least of its 8 chunks; otherwise it stays raw and reads need no decode.
